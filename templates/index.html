Project Introduction

This research investigates how machine learning can help users identify phishing and legitimate website URLs and email messages while improving cybersecurity awareness.

Participants will interact with a demonstration phishing detection system, and review its predictions and security explanations.

The system is designed for academic research purposes only. No real phishing websites will be accessed, and participants will not be asked to provide passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information.
