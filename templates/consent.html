Purpose of the research

This research investigates whether machine learning-based phishing detection tools can help users recognise phishing URLs and emails and support their cybersecurity decision-making.

What you will do

  1. Read the research information.
  2. Review the consent statements.
  3. Provide consent if you wish to participate.
  4. Access the PhisScan demonstration application.
  5. Test URL and email phishing detection using supplied simulated examples.
  6. Review the detection results and security explanations.
  7. Read the research debriefing.

Possible risks or discomfort

Participation involves minimal risk. Some simulated phishing examples may cause minor uncertainty or discomfort. No real malicious websites will be accessed.

Privacy and confidentiality

  • Participation is voluntary.
  • Please do not enter real passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (for example, the number of scans and their results) will be stored locally for research analysis.
  • These records are identified only by an anonymous participant ID. Your name, email address or signature are not collected.
  • The URLs and email text you analyse are not stored; only anonymous statistics (for example, that a scan took place and its result) are recorded.
  • Data will be handled according to the approved research protocol and applicable university data protection requirements.
