PhisScan Research Debriefing

Thank you for participating in this research.

This study investigates how machine learning-based phishing detection tools can support users in identifying potentially deceptive URLs and emails.

During this demonstration, you interacted with a prototype designed to classify submitted information as phishing or legitimate.

The application also provided explanations and cybersecurity advice to help users understand potential phishing indicators.

Machine learning systems are not always accurate. A legitimate-looking URL or email may still contain security risks, and legitimate content may occasionally be classified as suspicious.

The purpose of PhisScan is to support cybersecurity awareness and decision-making rather than replace independent security verification.

The anonymous usage statistics from your session will contribute to the evaluation of the prototype.

Thank you for contributing to this academic research.
